Clocks, Bells and the Working Day

Historians of work have long argued that industrialisation changed not only what people did, but how they experienced time. In farming communities the day was organised around tasks, and work stretched or shrank with the season.

The early mills had no such flexibility. Machines ran at a fixed pace, and owners needed workers present when they started. Bells, then clocks, then written rules followed.

By the 1830s, some mill owners were fining workers for every few minutes of lateness, and the clock rather than the task had become the measure of a day's work.

Schools and railways spread the same habits beyond the factory. By the end of the century punctuality was taught to children as a virtue.

Install it in a minute.

The first time you open Chalk, macOS will ask you to confirm. Here's what to click, and why.

  1. Download the .dmg

    Chalk_<version>_universal.dmg from the latest release. One file for Apple Silicon and Intel.

  2. Drag Chalk into Applications

    Open the .dmg and drag the icon across.

  3. Open it. macOS will block it.

    That's expected the first time.

  4. Click Open Anyway

    System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll down to "Chalk" was blocked to protect your Mac and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

  5. Allow notifications

    So deadlines and reminders reach you. Then press ⌘⇧K.

Why macOS warns you

Apple only fully trusts apps from developers who pay $99 a year and send every version to Apple to be checked ("notarized"). I'm a student and can't justify that for a free app, so Chalk isn't notarized.

The warning means Apple hasn't checked it. It doesn't mean anything is wrong with it. If I get a developer account, this step goes away.

Where are my todos stored?

On your Mac, in a local database. No account, nothing to sign in to. The folder is ~/Library/Application Support/com.cautiouslyfound.chalkv3.

How do I update?

Download the new .dmg and drag Chalk into Applications again, replacing the old one. Your todos and settings are kept.

How do I uninstall?

Quit Chalk from its menu bar icon and delete it from Applications. To remove your todos and settings too, delete the folder above.

Can it show in the Dock?

By default it lives only in the menu bar. Turn on Show in Dock in Settings (⌘S) and it appears in the Dock and ⌘Tab too.

What can I change in Settings?

Appearance (system, light or dark), font size from 12 to 20px, when deadline notifications arrive (at the deadline, a set time before, both, or none), notification sound, and whether the shortcut hints show in the footer.
